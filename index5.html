Our Philosophy

A Space Designed to
Make You Stay

Every material, every lamp, every cushion at Velvet Scoop was chosen with one intention — to create a space that feels as indulgent as our ice cream. Our interior design team spent over a year crafting an environment that transitions beautifully from the brightness of noon to the golden warmth of midnight.

Artisanal Interior Design
Hand-selected materials, bespoke furniture from local craftsmen, and a warm ochre-cream palette inspired by our flavours.
Intelligent Lighting System
Circadian-rhythm lighting adapts from bright natural tones at breakfast to warm amber for evening indulgence.
Curated Soundscapes
Acoustic engineers tuned each zone — jazz in the salon, nature sounds in the garden, gentle chatter in the café bar.

The Atmosphere

Designed to
Slow You Down

In a world that rushes, Velvet Scoop is a deliberate pause. Every sensory detail — from the temperature of the light to the weight of the silverware — is chosen to help you arrive, exhale, and simply be.

Experience It Yourself
Warm Lighting
2700K warm Edison bulbs and adjustable candlelight create a perpetual golden hour inside our walls.
Live Music
Thursday jazz evenings, Sunday acoustic sets, and a curated vinyl playlist every other day.

The Craft

Design Details That
Matter

01
Velvet Upholstery
All our booth seating is upholstered in Italian velvet sourced from Milan — deep burgundy, forest green, and midnight gold. Custom-made by a furniture studio in Jaipur.
02
Hand-Laid Terrazzo
Our signature gold-fleck terrazzo floors were laid by hand over six weeks. Each section is unique — no two panels are the same pattern, like snowflakes underfoot.
03
Arched Plasterwork
Each arch in the salon is hand-finished with lime plaster in three custom pigment shades — a warmth-layering technique used in traditional Lucknowi havelis.
