Our Philosophy

Why Velato is
Different

Three principles guide every batch we churn, every cup we pour, every space we design.

I
Origin Matters

We travel to the source — Ratnagiri for Alphonso mangoes, Belgium for couverture chocolate, Iran for pistachios. Each ingredient carries provenance. We carry that story into every scoop.

II
Made by Hand

No automated churners. No factory extracts. Our gelato artisans prepare every batch using traditional Italian pozzetti methods — slow-frozen, dense, and intensely flavoured.

III
Seasonal Honesty

Our menu changes with the harvest calendar. When strawberries peak, we feature them. When they're gone, they're gone — until next season. Honest flavour needs no shortcuts.

Our Story

From a Family
Kitchen to the City

"We don't sell dessert. We sell the feeling of a perfect moment — held in a small ceramic cup, at exactly the right temperature."

Velato began in 2010 as a single counter in old Hazratganj — three generations of recipes, finally shared with the world. Today we operate three locations and serve 50,000+ guests a year, but the kitchen process has not changed by a single step.

Every flavour is still tasted by our founder before it goes out. Every coffee is still pulled by hand. Every surface in our cafeteria is still chosen with obsessive care.
