Our Story

A Dream Churned into Reality

It all started in a small kitchen in 2009, with a grandmother's recipe and a whole lot of passion. Today, Scoops & Swirls is the city's most beloved destination for handcrafted ice creams and specialty coffee — where every visit feels like a warm hug.

100% Natural Ingredients
No artificial colours, no preservatives — only real fruits, premium dairy, and natural flavours.
Churned Fresh Daily
Every batch is prepared in our open kitchen — fresh every single morning, no exceptions.
Crafted with Love
Each scoop carries the warmth and care of our artisan team who live and breathe desserts.

Design & Interiors

Crafted with the Same Care as Our Scoops

Every detail of our space has been lovingly curated to feel magical from the moment you walk in.

Edison Warm Lighting
Over 300 hand-hung Edison bulbs cast a golden glow across exposed brick walls and reclaimed wood ceilings.
Hand-Painted Murals
Local artists commissioned to paint stunning ice cream dreamscapes across our 40-foot feature wall.
Living Plant Walls
Lush vertical gardens with 50+ tropical plant species purify the air and bring the outdoors inside.
Curated Soundscape
In-house DJ curates lo-fi beats and jazz — live performances every Friday & Saturday evening.
Bespoke Furniture
Every chair and booth custom-crafted by Lucknow artisans using sustainably sourced teak and velvet.
Evening Candlelight
As the sun sets, lighting transitions to warm candlelight across every table for an intimate glow.
Eco-Conscious Design
Solar panels, rainwater harvesting and zero single-use plastic — beautiful and kind to the planet.
Photo-Ready Corners
Six dedicated Instagram corners with ring-light setups, neon signs and floral backdrops.
