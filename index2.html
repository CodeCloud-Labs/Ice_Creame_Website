Our Sweet Story

Crafted from a
Family Recipe

Born in a small kitchen in old Lucknow, Scoopery has grown from a beloved family secret into the city's most celebrated gelato destination. Three generations of recipes, perfected over 15 years.

We source Alphonso mangoes from Ratnagiri, Belgian couverture chocolate, ceremonial matcha & single-origin Colombian coffee — because every flavour deserves the finest beginning.
