Crafted From a Family Recipe, Dripping with Love

Born in a small Lucknow kitchen in 2010, Drip started as a family obsession — three generations of ice cream lore, finally shared with the world. Every batch is still prepared by hand, every flavour still personally approved by our founder before it reaches your bowl.

We source Alphonso mangoes from Ratnagiri, Belgian chocolate from Bruges, pistachios from Iran, and single-origin coffee from Colombia — because great gelato begins before the churn.
